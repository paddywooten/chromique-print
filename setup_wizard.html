Firebase Auth Setup Wizard

4 steps, top to bottom. One single rules paste. Green means go.

Run this once, on your own computer. Don't publish this page to your live website.

1 Enable Email/Password Sign-In

Open the page below, click Email/Password, flip the first toggle to Enable, and press Save.

Open Sign-In Providers Page
If the button doesn't open: https://console.firebase.google.com/project/chromique-print/authentication/providers

2 Publish the Security Rules (one paste, done forever)

Type your email (the director's) and click Generate. Your email is written into the rules as the founder account — the only one allowed to set itself up as the first manager. Then copy, paste over everything on the rules page, and Publish. That's the only rules paste you'll ever do.

Open Firestore Rules Page
If the button doesn't open: https://console.firebase.google.com/project/chromique-print/firestore/rules
Show the rules (click inside the box to select everything)

Tip: click anywhere inside the box — the whole text gets selected — then press Ctrl+C (Cmd+C on Mac).

After publishing: verify the rules

The wizard probes your live database and confirms every rule behaves correctly — public blocked from reading orders, junk writes rejected, customer checkout still working. Add your manager login (optional) to also test manager access and auto-clean the test order.

3 Your Manager Account

Already tried creating it? Check first

This tells you exactly what state your account is in: does it exist, does the password work, and is the manager role attached.

Create Manager (you — the director)

Safe to re-run: if the account already exists it just adds the missing role. Other managers (e.g. Jessica) are added later from the dashboard → Manage Team Accounts.

4 Verify Everything Works

The wizard signs in, checks your role, and confirms order data is accessible under the rules.

🎉

Setup Complete!

Sign in to the Admin Dashboard and add your team (staff & managers) from Manage Team Accounts. You can close and delete this wizard page.
